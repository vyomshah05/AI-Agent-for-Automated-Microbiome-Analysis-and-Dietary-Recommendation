# MICROBIOME HEALTH REPORT

## Sample Summary
This report analyzes sample AG_S011 from a 29-year-old female omnivore with a normal BMI.

## Community Composition
The gut microbial community of sample AG_S011 is dominated by bacteria from the phylum Bacteroidetes, specifically the genera *Parabacteroides*, *Prevotella*, *Alistipes*, and *Bacteroides*. These microbes are generally known for their roles in breaking down complex carbohydrates and producing short-chain fatty acids.

## Diversity Assessment vs. Cohort Reference Ranges
The alpha diversity of this sample indicates a dysbiotic state. The Shannon diversity index is 3.16, which is below the healthy cohort's low reference range of 3.62 (mean 3.92, high 4.23). The Firmicutes/Bacteroidetes ratio is notably low at 0.08, compared to the cohort's mean of 6.50. While the observed OTUs (106) and Chao1 index (106) fall within the cohort's reference ranges (105.46-111.87 and 102.40-120.22, respectively), the significantly reduced Shannon diversity and F/B ratio are key indicators of dysbiosis.

## Top 3 Flagged Taxa with Brief Context
The following genera were found at unusually high relative abundance compared to the cohort:

*   ***Parabacteroides*** (13.17% relative abundance vs. cohort mean 1.37%): Some species of *Parabacteroides*, particularly *P. distasonis*, have been studied for their potential probiotic effects, protecting against inflammation and obesity, and their ability to metabolize dietary components like glycitein (PMID: 35945336, 2022; PMID: 39260371, 2024). However, other studies suggest depletion of *P. distasonis* by high-fat diets is associated with increased colorectal tumorigenesis (PMID: 34461052, 2022).
*   ***Prevotella*** (12.77% relative abundance vs. cohort mean 1.41%): *Prevotella* species are known to thrive on diets rich in fermentable carbohydrates and some species, like *P. copri*, have been linked to favorable postprandial glucose metabolism (PMID: 32475524, 2020; PMID: 33432175, 2021). Conversely, *P. copri* has also been implicated in exacerbating atherosclerosis through trimethylamine production (PMID: 38777572, 2024).
*   ***Alistipes*** (11.37% relative abundance vs. cohort mean 1.77%): *Alistipes* species are considered bile-tolerant microorganisms. High abundance of *Alistipes* has been observed with animal-based diets and can be negatively correlated with cardiometabolic health (PMID: 24336217, 2014; PMID: 39762435, 2025). Some studies show a decrease in *Alistipes* with beneficial interventions like *Akkermansia muciniphila* administration (PMID: 37377410, 2023).

## Dietary Recommendations
Based on the high abundance of Bacteroidetes, including *Prevotella*, and low diversity, consider the following:

*   **Increase intake of diverse plant-based fibers:** Focus on a wide variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and legumes to support overall microbial diversity and provide substrates for beneficial bacteria (PMID: 32475524, 2020).
*   **Limit red meat and processed foods:** High consumption of red meat can be associated with increased *Alistipes* and negative cardiometabolic outcomes (PMID: 39762435, 2025).
*   **Consider fermentable carbohydrates:** As *Prevotella* thrives on these, incorporating resistant starches and other complex carbohydrates may be beneficial, but monitor for individual responses.
*   **Maintain a balanced fat intake:** Avoid very high-fat diets, which can alter microbial composition and favor bile-resistant organisms, potentially impacting inflammatory processes (PMID: 24336217, 2014; PMID: 34461052, 2022).

## Probiotic Suggestions
Given the potential benefits of *Parabacteroides distasonis* in maintaining gut homeostasis and its association with reduced inflammation and obesity, a targeted probiotic containing this species may be considered, if available and appropriate (PMID: 35945336, 2022).

## Overall Microbiome Health Score: 4/10
The microbiome health score is 4/10 due to significantly low Shannon diversity and a very low Firmicutes/Bacteroidetes ratio, indicating a state of dysbiosis, despite having a stable number of observed OTUs.
